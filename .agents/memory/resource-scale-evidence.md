---
name: Resource-scale evidence rules
description: Approval scope and evidence standards for the data-center resource reading prototype
---

Resource-scale case studies stay in the sandbox unless their integration is separately approved. Research is deliberately curated, not a generic scraping service.

**Why:** The requested prototype is an evidence-reading experiment, not an approved addition to the main biology workspace or an automated collection product.

**How to apply:** Extend the existing sandbox reading when adding evidence. Do not move it into the main product or add backend scraping without separate approval.

An unknown baseline or accounting boundary is a reason to withhold a comparison, not to supply a plausible substitute. A shared unit alone does not make different projects' figures comparable.

**Why:** Announced generation capacity is not facility electricity load, and projected water “use” does not establish either withdrawal or net consumption. Operational phases and reporting years can differ even when the units match.

**How to apply:** Establish source dates, phase, physical scope and measurement definitions first. Keep unavailable values explicit. Preserve withdrawal, discharge, net consumption and replenishment as separate categories; do not rank incompatible footprints.
